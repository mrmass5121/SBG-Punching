Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before placing an order with S.B.G. Punching.

What materials do you cut and punch?

We work with Mild Steel (MS), Stainless Steel (SS 304 & 316), Aluminium, GI (Galvanised Iron), Copper, and Brass sheets. Our fiber laser handles all these materials up to 20mm thickness, while our CNC turret punch covers 0.8mm to 3mm.

What is the minimum order quantity?

There is no minimum order quantity. We accept single-piece prototype orders as well as large bulk production runs. Pricing is competitive and adjusted based on volume — contact us for a free quote.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Standard orders are completed and ready within 2–5 working days depending on complexity, quantity, and material availability. Urgent or rush jobs can often be accommodated — call us on +91 88921 81792 to confirm availability.

What thickness can your laser cut?

Our fiber laser cutting machine handles up to 20mm thickness on Mild Steel and Stainless Steel, and up to 15mm on Aluminium. For very thin gauge work (0.8mm–3mm), CNC turret punching is more economical and equally precise.

Do you fabricate custom control panel enclosures?

Yes — it's one of our core specialisations. We fabricate custom electrical control panel enclosures with precision-punched cutouts, cable gland holes, DIN rail mounting slots, and ventilation perforations for switchgear, automation, MCC panels, and power distribution boards.

Do you offer job work for OEMs and manufacturers?

Yes. We are a preferred job work partner for OEMs, contract manufacturers, and engineering workshops across Bangalore and Karnataka. We handle CNC punching, laser cutting, bending, and fabrication job works with consistent precision and fast delivery.

Do you do bar bending and angle bending work?

Yes. We handle bar bending, angle bending, plate bending, and profile bending using our CNC press brake. We work with MS, SS, and aluminium bars and angles. This is a common requirement for structural fabrication, industrial frames, and support structures — contact us with your dimensions for a quick quote.

Where are you located and do you serve clients across Bangalore?

Our facility is at Nadakerappa Industrial Estate, Hegganahalli, Bangalore – 560091. We supply precision sheet metal components to clients across Bangalore including Peenya, Sunkadakatte, Rajajinagar, Yeshwanthpur, Tumkur Road, Bommasandra, Whitefield, Electronic City, and across Karnataka.

How do I get a quote?

The fastest way is to WhatsApp us your drawing, DXF file, or a description of your requirement. You can also use the enquiry form below or call us directly. We respond within a few hours on working days.
